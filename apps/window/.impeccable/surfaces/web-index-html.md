## Scope and mode

Dedicated Frank route `/ad-radar`; Operate mode. The surface covers research setup, live run observation, the approved public creative library, evidence inspection, comparison, quarantine recovery, and publish approval.

## Audience and job

Steven or another trusted operator needs to see what changed in public advertising, find useful creative quickly, and verify exactly why any item can or cannot publish. Primary tasks are run research, inspect attention, compare creatives, and approve an immutable release.

## Proof and constraints

Every displayed creative must resolve to a public source observation, classification/media-QA evidence, and durable Hermes receipts. Frank only validates and renders; Hermes executes and persists. Synthetic demonstration content is explicitly labelled. No contact/outreach data, private prompts, provider payloads, or filesystem references cross the boundary.

## Chosen direction

Observation Timeline is the default workspace: a precise chronological roll of new, changed, stopped, quarantined, approved, and published creatives with a fixed evidence inspector. Creative Fieldbook becomes the Library view, preserving visual search and a pinned compare tray.

## Memorable moment

Selecting a change reveals the creative and its complete provenance in place while the compare tray remains pinned, so chronology and deliberate comparison never compete.

## Unresolved decisions

Production source credentials and the exact first scheduled cadence remain deployment configuration, not UI defaults.
